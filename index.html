    About Real-time Coverage

    This information is an estimate based on train locations from Iarnród Éireann's central signalling system and scheduled journey times. Trains indicated as late can make up time. Real-time coverage is limited in some areas:

    • Athlone - Westport/Ballina Line
    • Cork Station & Cork - Cobh/Midleton Line
    • Mallow - Tralee Line
    • Ballybrophy - Limerick Line
    • Limerick - Ennis Line & Limerick Jct - Waterford Line
    • Greystones - Rosslare Line
    • Dundalk - Belfast Line

    In these areas, scheduled times are shown instead.
